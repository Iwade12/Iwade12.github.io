About me 


My name is Ila Wade, and I’m a photographer with a background in film and a minor in photography. Originally from Colorado and currently based in South Carolina, I specialize in capturing authentic moments through a creative and intentional lens


My work reflects a blend of technical skill and artistic perspective, shaped by my academic training and personal passion for visual storytelling. Whether working with natural landscapes, portraits, or editorial concepts, I aim to deliver high-quality, compelling images that communicate meaning and emotion.
